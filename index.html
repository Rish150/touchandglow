Good to know

Questions before your appointment?

We've gathered the essentials. If you need anything else, call or send us a note — we're happy to help.

How should I prepare for waxing?+

Let hair grow to about 1/4 inch, gently exfoliate the day before, and arrive with clean, product-free skin. Avoid tanning and strong exfoliants beforehand.

Is threading suitable for sensitive skin?+

Threading uses cotton thread and no wax or chemicals, making it a popular choice for sensitive skin. Tell your specialist about any sensitivities before your service.

How often should I book?+

Most guests return every 3–5 weeks for threading or waxing. Facial and lash timing depends on the treatment and your personal goals.

Do you offer services for men?+

Yes. Our men's grooming menu includes threading and waxing for brows, face, chest, back, arms, legs, and more.
